License text catalog provenance
===============================

This directory contains selectable license texts for the user's original
Python source. These files do not replace the separate license notices for
pytors and its bundled runtime or other third-party material.

Source texts were checked against the SPDX License List and the authoritative
license-publisher URLs below on 2026-10-27. Apache-2.0, MPL-2.0, and the GNU
licenses were copied from /usr/share/common-licenses, preserving their full
terms and application appendices. GPL/LGPL publisher URLs were unavailable
through the web reader during verification; their SPDX entries and the local
standard texts supplied the corresponding versioned texts.

In MIT, 0BSD, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, and Zlib, the replaceable software
copyright-holder field identifies "the authors of the original Python source"
instead of attributing the user's source to the license's example authors.
WTFPL's notice credits the author of the license document and is unchanged.
BSL-1.0 has no software copyright-holder placeholder in its standard text.
The Apache, MPL, and GNU license-document copyrights and example application
instructions remain unchanged.

The -only and -or-later choices share the same versioned GNU license body;
the chosen SPDX identifier in the source's licensing notice selects the
version option. Example notices in the standard application appendices are
part of those unmodified license documents, not extra notices for the user's
source. Each LGPL-3.0 resource includes both the LGPL-3.0 supplement and the
full GPL-3.0 text that it incorporates.

Whitespace wrapping and software copyright-holder substitutions in the short
license templates follow SPDX's matching guidelines:
https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v2.3/license-matching-guidelines-and-templates/
